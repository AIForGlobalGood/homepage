# Code of Conduct

AI For Global Good adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its Code of Conduct for every space it runs — this repository, our website, chat channels, events, and any project led under our name.

The short version:

- **Be kind.** Assume good faith. Disagree without being disagreeable.
- **Be inclusive.** We welcome people of every background, geography, and level of experience, especially from communities that are often excluded.
- **Be honest.** About what you know, what you don't, what works, and what doesn't.
- **No harassment.** Of any kind, in any form, in any space we run.
- **Communities first.** When we work with partners — especially in low- and middle-income settings — their safety, dignity, and consent come before our convenience.

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code, please report it. Until we have a dedicated address, email the founders listed in [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md) or open a confidential issue by emailing the maintainers directly.

Reports are handled seriously, promptly, and confidentially. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation.

For the full text, scope, enforcement guidelines, and definitions, read the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Where the full text and this summary conflict, the full text applies.
